The application uses **simulated mission data**. Astronauts see their own private health data; the flight surgeon cannot access their BLUE conversations or psychological reports. BLUE adjusts its questions to the person's profile and replies. Ending a session generates a structured report marked **“Queued for Earth.”** The film does not demonstrate a live wearable feed or an actual Earth transmission, so the copy avoids those claims. The footage shows a light interface against a midnight-blue film background, while `AGENTS.md` specifies a fully night-navy console; this visual difference is relevant if the film is revised later.
