# Project BLUE: Astronaut Physical & Psychological Health Platform

**Track:** NASA Space Apps Challenge — Astronaut Health Monitoring

**Interface Aesthetic:** Clinical Light Blue & White Medical Aerospace UI

**AI Companion:** **BLUE** (Behavioral & Longitudinal Unified Evaluation)

## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Problem

### The Deep-Space Psychological & Physiological Challenge

Long-duration missions to the Lunar Gateway and Mars place unprecedented strain on both the astronaut's body and mind. Microgravity alters cardiovascular regulation, glucose metabolism, and circadian cortisol rhythms, while prolonged isolation, confinement, and high-stakes workloads elevate risks of anxiety, sleep disruption, and cognitive fatigue.

Because deep-space communication latency reaches **up to 20+ minutes one-way**, astronauts cannot hold real-time therapy sessions with flight psychologists on Earth. Meanwhile, traditional spacecraft medical interfaces dump raw telemetry charts without connecting how an astronaut's *physical state* affects their *mental well-being*—or translating check-ins into structured clinical briefs for Earth-based specialists.

### Our Solution

**Project BLUE** is a unified, light-blue and white clinical web application that bridges daily biometric tracking with empathetic, adaptive psychological support:

1. **Astronaut Identity Selection:** Crew members select their astronaut profile upon entry to load personalized longitudinal biometrics, mission context, and psychological history.
2. **Spatial 3-Column Health Dashboard:**
   * **Left Panel (Physical Telemetry):** Interactive charts and live metrics for Heart Rate, Blood Sugar (Glucose), Blood Pressure, Blood Oxygen ($\text{SpO}_2$), and Cortisol.
   * **Center Stage (Interactive Human Model):** An anatomical human body visualization linking physical vitals and psychological stress markers directly to physiological systems, adapting dynamically when switching between the **Physical Panel** and **Mental Panel** views.
   * **Right Panel (Mental Health Reports & Earth Link):** A longitudinal log of psychological check-in summaries, risk assessments, and actionable recommendations sent back from Earth's flight psychologist.
3. **Conversational Voice AI Psychologist ("BLUE"):** Triggered via a round floating voice button in the bottom-right corner, **BLUE** speaks and listens like a calm, perceptive human psychologist. It tailors its questioning depth to the astronaut's current physical and emotional state—conducting a quick check-in when everything looks steady, or asking thoughtful follow-up questions when biomarkers or vocal cues suggest strain—and automatically writes a structured **Summary Report for Earth** so a real psychologist can review the case and prescribe next steps.

## 2. Core User Journey & Functional Pillars

### Step 1: Astronaut Crew Selector

* Upon opening the web app, the user chooses which astronaut they are (e.g., *Cmdr. Elena Vance*, *Dr. Marcus Chen*, *Spec. Maya Lin*).
* Each profile loads distinct baseline physiological trends, current mission day, and previous mental health reports so judges can test both nominal and high-stress crew scenarios.

### Step 2: Unified Dashboard with Physical / Mental Panel Switcher

* **Top Control Bar:** Displays the active astronaut's profile, mission day, Earth communication delay indicator, and a prominent **`Physical Panel` | `Mental Panel`** mode switcher.
  * **Physical Panel Focus:** Emphasizes cardiovascular, metabolic, and respiratory chart detail on the left and highlights organ/system nodes (heart, bloodstream/glucose, lungs) on the central human body model.
  * **Mental Panel Focus:** Highlights neurological/cortisol stress pathways on the central human model, surfaces cognitive load & mood trends, and expands the Earth psychologist report detail on the right.
* **Left Column — Physiological Charts & Telemetry:** Clean time-series line/area charts and current readouts for **Heartbeat (BPM)**, **Blood Sugar ($\text{mg/dL}$)**, **Blood Pressure ($\text{mmHg}$)**, **$\text{SpO}_2$ ($\%$)**, and **Cortisol ($\mu\text{g/dL}$)**.
* **Center Column — Interactive Human Model:** Clean anatomical silhouette in light blue and white with clickable/status-aware body zones showing real-time physical and stress indicators.
* **Right Column — Mental Health Reports:** Displays chronological psychological summary reports generated by BLUE, Earth transmission status (`QUEUED FOR EARTH`, `REVIEWED BY EARTH PSYCHOLOGIST`), and clinical guidance from Earth.

### Step 3: Bottom-Right Voice AI Psychologist ("BLUE")

* **Trigger:** A round floating action button in the bottom-right corner opens the **BLUE Voice Session** interface.
* **Human-Like Adaptive Interviewing:**
  * **Context-Aware Opening:** Before asking its first question, BLUE reads the astronaut's active vitals and recent reports (e.g., noticing a drop in blood sugar or elevated evening cortisol).
  * **Dynamic Follow-Up Depth:**
    * *Nominal Situation:* Keeps the conversation light, supportive, and brief (2–3 questions) before wrapping up.
    * *Elevated Physical/Mental Strain:* Gently asks targeted follow-up questions (3–5 turns) about sleep quality, workload pressure, physical symptoms, and emotional state without sounding robotic or alarmist.
* **Automated Earth Psychologist Summary Report:**
  * At the end of the conversation, BLUE compiles a structured clinical brief containing: *Session Date/Mission Day*, *Subjective Mood & Key Themes*, *Correlated Biometric Context*, *AI Triage Observation*, and *Recommended Action / Questions for Earth Psychologist*.
  * The new report immediately appears at the top of the **Right Column (Mental Health Reports)** ready for Earth uplink, with an option in the demo to simulate receiving the Earth psychologist's response instructions.

## 3. Data Strategy & Open Sources

* **Physiological Baselines:** Curated realistic astronaut curves inspired by **PhysioNet** (heart rate, blood pressure, glucose, $\text{SpO}_2$) and **NASA OSDR** (spaceflight cortisol and circadian adaptation profiles).
* **Stress & Affect Calibration:** Informed by **WESAD** multimodal stress patterns to link physical markers (cortisol + heart rate variability) with psychological check-in prompts.
* **Clinical Report Structure:** Modeled on aerospace behavioral health check-in standards (**NASA-STD-3001** Behavioral Health & Performance guidelines).

## 4. Hackathon Scope: 48-Hour MVP vs. Future Vision

### In Scope (48-Hour Hackathon Deliverable)

1. **Astronaut Selection Screen:** 3–4 selectable astronaut personas with distinct physical and psychological states.
2. **Light Blue & White 3-Column Crew Dashboard:**
   * Top switcher between **Physical Panel** and **Mental Panel**.
   * Left column interactive charts (Heartbeat, Blood Sugar, Blood Pressure, $\text{SpO}_2$, Cortisol).
   * Center interactive anatomical **Human Model** linked to live metrics.
   * Right column **Mental Health Reports** feed with Earth transmission status and psychologist directives.
3. **Bottom-Right Round Voice AI Button ("BLUE"):**
   * Live speech-to-text + natural voice output (with text-chat fallback for noisy demo rooms).
   * Adaptive psychologist conversation logic that scales follow-up questions based on astronaut state.
   * Automatic generation of an **Earth Summary Report** saved directly into the right-hand panel.

### Out of Scope (Post-Hackathon Roadmap)

* **Hands-Free Wake Word (`"Hey Blue"`):** Planned for v2 so astronauts can initiate sessions hands-free while floating in microgravity; strictly excluded from the 48-hour MVP in favor of the reliable bottom-right round button.
* **Live Wearable Hardware Integration:** Direct Bluetooth/telemetry ingestion from Astroskin or Bio-Monitor flight hardware.
* **Real Deep Space Network (DSN) Uplink:** Live ground-control clinician portal authentication and satellite routing.
